All sources available under CC0. Trimmed, normalized and converted to mono 44.1kHz PCM WAV for iPad compatibility.

Pistol: The Free Firearm Sound Library — Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras, Matthew Nanney. Walther PPQ X_31P (first shot).
https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library

Reload and holster handling: SpringySpringo, Gun reload sounds (recorded airsoft handling). Reload shortened to match 1.5 seconds; holster is a short handling excerpt.
https://opengameart.org/content/gun-reload-sounds

Footsteps: Fantozzi, Stone left/right variants.
https://opengameart.org/content/fantozzis-footsteps-grasssand-stone

Rain: alxl, Rain on Window Loop.
https://opengameart.org/content/rain-on-window-loop

Door open: themightyglider, derived from forseti1121.
https://opengameart.org/content/iron-door

Door close and power mechanism: rubberduck, 100 CC0 SFX.
https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-sfx

Switch: TinyWorlds, stove switch.
https://opengameart.org/content/stove-switch

Surface variants: carpet uses softened/filtered recorded stone steps; maintenance metal uses a short room resonance treatment; wet paving mixes recorded stone steps with rubberduck splash_02 (CC0, 100 CC0 SFX). These are adapted recordings, not recordings of each exact surface.

v0.12: pistol-v12.wav is the same CC0 recorded Walther PPQ shot, shortened to its direct attack with additional low/mid weight. Runtime room-specific convolution adds early reflections and damped tails; outside has very little tail.
