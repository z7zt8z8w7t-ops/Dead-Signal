THE INVESTIGATION

Arrive outside in the rain. Explore a clean, brightly lit reception, offices and corridors. Find the power test switch in maintenance to cut the mains and activate emergency lights. No creatures in this test. Recorded rain, footsteps, doors and weapon effects start when you press Start. SOUND ON / OFF toggles audio.

Left stick moves and faces the direction you push. MAP above the stick opens and closes the explored map. DRAW / HOLSTER below the stick toggles the Glock 17 and its torch. The vest light gives a softer glow nearby.

USE above FIRE opens doors and operates the power switch. RELOAD below FIRE refills the 17-round magazine from unlimited reserves. You can reload early and move during the 1.5-second reload.

Keyboard: WASD / arrows move · E use · H draw / holster · R reload · Space fire · M map · Escape pause. The switch toggles both ways for visual comparison.
