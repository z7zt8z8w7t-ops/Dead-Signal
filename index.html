DEAD SIGNAL

Explore the centre, open doors and shoot the creatures. 12-round magazine, unlimited reserves. Reload whenever you need to.

Left stick moves and turns you. FIRE shoots forward. RELOAD takes 1.5 seconds; you can move while reloading. MAP pauses the action and shows explored areas plus your objective. USE opens/closes nearby doors and operates terminals. Brown blocks stop movement and shots. Red doors are locked.

Keyboard: WASD or arrows to move and face, Space to fire, E to use, R to reload, M for map, Escape to pause.
