DEAD SIGNAL

Explore the centre and shoot the creatures. Unlimited ammunition; no reloads.

Left stick moves and turns you. FIRE shoots forward. MAP pauses the action and shows explored areas plus your objective. USE operates nearby terminals.

Keyboard: WASD or arrows to move and face, Space to fire, E to use, M for map, Escape to pause.
